Job market for senior plus

🌿 Growingest.further readingStarted 12 Aug 20263 min read#career#market

Inspired by Career advice in 2025.

Before 2022

The fun of being an engineer

The priority was to ensure engineers are looked after, and consulted for decisions.

From The good times in tech are over

For most of the last decade, being a software engineer has been a lot of fun. Every company offered lots of perks, layoffs and firings were almost unheard of, and in general we were treated as special little geniuses who needed to be pampered so we could work our magic. That’s changed in the last two years.

From Career advice in 2025.

Many people who first entered senior roles in 2010-2020 are finding current roles a lot less fun

Related: Programming is fun

A spike of new engineers

Because it was so fun, it brought a new wave of engineers.

Business priorities

The priority was to acquire users, not make money.

From The good times in tech are over

Tech companies definitely did not have to be profitable. In fact, they didn’t even need to make money — they just had to acquire users, or at least hype, to drive up the valuation of the company itself. In that environment, throwing money at their software engineers (in the form of paid trips, in-house chefs, and huge comp packages) was a sensible business decision.

Your priorities vs the company's priorities:

If you were an engineer who loved working on your company’s open-source libraries, it’s probably sensible to confront the fact that the company never really cared about it that much. When interest rates were zero, it was worth doing because most things were worth doing. At 5% interest rates, most open-source work doesn’t meet that bar. In other words, your interests now conflict with your company’s interests.

Relationships with companies

From The good times in tech are over

During the 2010s, it was as if companies were their software engineers, and were interested in the same things as their engineers were. A lot of engineers were fooled by this into identifying strongly with their employer.
Now the mirage has vanished. Companies are their executive leadership, and their executive leadership are interested in a much smaller set of things.

This hits home.
We used to wonder if a company was a "tech-first" company.

What happened in 2022

ZIRP - Zero Interest Rate Policy

From The good times in tech are over

In the 2010s, interest rates were zero or close to zero2. Investors could thus borrow a lot of money. Much of that money was spent on tech companies in the hope of outsized returns. Tech companies were thus incentivized to (a) hire like crazy, and (b) do a lot of low-risk high-reward things, even if that ends up wasting money.

After 2022

Summary from Blog - The good times in tech are over:

    • Providing value to the company gets you rewarded
    • Not providing value to the company gets you punished
    • “Value to the company” means furthering the explicit plans of your company’s executives

Lots of candidates

From Career advice in 2025.

hiring managers need some way to filter through the higher volume of candidates.
if you’re finding this job market difficult, it’s certainly not personal.

Your interests vs the company's interests

From The good times in tech are over

when you act in ways that don’t further your company’s interests, you risk being seen as ineffective or unreliable. In 2025, that makes you vulnerable to being laid off.

Definitely felt that one.

New focus of companies

Profitable: making money is now important.

From The good times in tech are over

companies now are actually trying to focus. In 2015, there was a lot of appetite to do everything at the same time
In 2025, most of these initiatives have been abruptly defunded in order to put more resources into a handful of bets that the company executives actually care about.

Impact of AI

From The good times in tech are over

I do believe in the power of AI, and I wouldn’t be surprised if it takes software jobs at some point in the future, but it’s certainly not behind the vibe shift in software engineering right now.
